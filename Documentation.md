# Mini Game Team 5

## Game Idea: Tag Game
<ul>
<li> One Player is the Tagger and the other is the Runner. The Runner needs to avoid the Tagger. </li>
 <li> The Tagger wins if they touch the Runner, while the Runner wins when the Timer runs out (30 Seconds). </li>
 <li> 1 Time Dash to help avoid opponent (Or Timer for Dash Cool Down) </li>
<li>Physical Obstacles they can run behind</li>  
<li>Tagger is Faster than Runner</li> 
<li>Runner needs to be strategic about when they use the Dash and the Tagger needs to try and cut them off</li> 

</ul>

## Objective Statement
The Runner will feel Tension as they attempt to avoid the Tagger, feeling Relief when the timer runs out and when they narrowly avoid the Tagger.

The Tagger will feel Frustrated whenever they do not catch the Runner, feeling Joy whenever they catch the Runner.
